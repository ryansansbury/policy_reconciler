# The judge never grades its own family

## The decision

The judge that grades drafts always comes from a different vendor than the drafter, and routing refuses any route that breaks this.

## Why

A model can favor writing that reads like its own, and that bias is hard to see from the scores alone. Separating the families removes it by construction.

## What it costs

A client that approves only one vendor can't use the judge, and the routing panel shows that case. Every criterion then stays with a person.
